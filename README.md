<h1><span class="s1"><strong>IT Service Management and Ticketing with Jira</strong></span></h1>
<p class="p3">A practical IT Support portfolio focused on Level 1 (L1) Service Desk operations using Jira Service Management.</p>
<p class="p3">This project demonstrates my understanding of ticket management, incident classification, prioritization, troubleshooting documentation, user communication, and ticket resolution through simulated IT support scenarios.</p>
<p class="p3"><strong>Platform:</strong> Jira Service Management Cloud<br /> <span class="s1"><strong>Project Type:</strong></span> Personal IT Support Lab<br /> <span class="s1"><strong>Support Level:</strong></span> Level 1 (L1)<br /> <span class="s1"><strong>Case Studies:</strong></span> 10 simulated scenarios<br /> <span class="s1"><strong>Documentation Language:</strong></span> English<br /> <span class="s1"><strong>Status:</strong></span> In Progress</p>
<p class="p4">All cases are simulated for educational and portfolio purposes. They do not represent professional support work performed for real customers or employers.</p>
<hr />
<h2><span class="s1"><strong>1. Project Overview</strong></span></h2>
<p class="p3">This project demonstrates my understanding of IT Service Desk operations through a collection of simulated support incidents and service requests managed using Jira Service Management.</p>
<p class="p3">The scenarios are based on common technical issues encountered by IT Support Technicians and Service Desk Analysts.</p>
<p class="p3">The main objective is to demonstrate the fundamentals of managing a support ticket from the initial user report through investigation, troubleshooting, resolution, and closure.</p>
<p class="p5">The project focuses on:</p>
<ul>
<li>Incident management</li>
<li>Service request management</li>
<li>Ticket categorization and prioritization</li>
<li>Ticket assignment and status management</li>
<li>User communication and ticket updates</li>
<li>Level 1 troubleshooting</li>
<li>Resolution documentation</li>
<li>Escalation procedures</li>
<li>Ticket lifecycle management</li>
</ul>
<hr />
<h2><span class="s1"><strong>2. Project Objectives</strong></span></h2>
<p class="p5">The objectives of this project are to:</p>
<ol start="1">
<li>Understand how an IT Service Desk manages incidents and service requests.</li>
<li>Create, categorize, and prioritize support tickets.</li>
<li>Follow a structured troubleshooting process.</li>
<li>Document investigation steps and technical findings.</li>
<li>Communicate progress through ticket updates.</li>
<li>Record solutions and verify outcomes before closing tickets.</li>
<li>Recognize when escalation to Level 2 support is necessary.</li>
<li>Gain practical experience with Jira Service Management.</li>
<li>Build a portfolio demonstrating foundational IT Support skills.</li>
</ol>

<h2><span class="s1"><strong>4. Ticket Lifecycle</strong></span></h2>
<p class="p4">The simulated workflow follows a typical IT Service Desk process:</p>
<ol start="1">
<li><span class="s1"><strong>Ticket Creation:</strong></span> Record the user’s issue or service request.</li>
<li><span class="s1"><strong>Classification:</strong></span> Identify the type and category of the ticket.</li>
<li><span class="s1"><strong>Prioritization:</strong></span> Assess urgency and impact.</li>
<li><span class="s1"><strong>Investigation:</strong></span> Perform initial troubleshooting.</li>
<li><span class="s1"><strong>Documentation:</strong></span> Record actions, findings, and communication.</li>
<li><span class="s1"><strong>Resolution or Escalation:</strong></span> Apply a verified solution or escalate the issue when necessary.</li>
<li><span class="s1"><strong>Closure:</strong></span> Document the final outcome and complete the ticket lifecycle.</li>
</ol>
<p class="p1">The actual status names and transitions depend on the Jira workflow configured for the project.</p>

<h3><span class="s1"><strong>Case 1 — Internet Connectivity Issue</strong></span></h3>
<p class="p3"><strong>Ticket ID:</strong><span class="s2"> IT-001<br /> </span><span class="s1"><strong>Type:</strong></span><span class="s2"> Incident<br /> </span><span class="s1"><strong>Category:</strong></span><span class="s2"> Network / Connectivity</span></p>
<p class="p3"><strong>Scenario</strong></p>
<p class="p4">A user reports that their Windows 11 computer cannot access the internet. The problem started during the working day and is preventing access to online services.</p>
<p class="p5"><strong>Troubleshooting activities</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Check the network connection.</li>
<li>Review the IP configuration using <code>ipconfig /all</code>.</li>
<li>Test connectivity to the default gateway.</li>
<li>Test external network connectivity where appropriate.</li>
<li>Check DNS resolution using <code>nslookup</code>.</li>
</ul>
<p class="p3"><strong>Expected outcome</strong></p>
<p class="p4">Identify the likely cause, document the findings, apply an appropriate corrective action if within Level 1 scope, and verify connectivity.</p>
<p class="p4">If the issue requires network-level investigation beyond the technician’s permissions, escalate it to the appropriate team.</p>
<p class="p4"><strong>Skills demonstrated:</strong> TCP/IP fundamentals, DNS troubleshooting, network diagnostics, incident management.</p>
<h3><span class="s1"><strong>Case 2 — User Account Locked</strong></span></h3>
<p class="p3"><strong>Ticket ID:</strong><span class="s2"> IT-002<br /> </span><span class="s1"><strong>Type:</strong></span><span class="s2"> Incident<br /> </span><span class="s1"><strong>Category:</strong></span><span class="s2"> Account / Access</span></p>
<p class="p3"><strong>Scenario</strong></p>
<p class="p4">A user cannot sign in to their Windows workstation because their account appears to be locked after multiple unsuccessful login attempts.</p>
<p class="p5"><strong>Troubleshooting activities</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Confirm the exact sign-in error.</li>
<li>Identify the affected account.</li>
<li>Check account status when authorized.</li>
<li>Follow the organization’s identity-verification procedure.</li>
<li>Unlock the account only when authorized.</li>
</ul>
<p class="p3"><strong>Expected outcome</strong></p>
<p class="p4">Restore access when authorized, verify that the user can sign in, and document the action taken.</p>
<p class="p4">Escalate if administrator intervention or further security investigation is required.</p>
<p class="p4"><strong>Skills demonstrated:</strong> Account troubleshooting, authentication, access management, security awareness.</p>
<h3><span class="s1"><strong>Case 3 — Password Reset Request</strong></span></h3>
<p class="p3"><strong>Ticket ID:</strong><span class="s2"> IT-003<br /> </span><span class="s1"><strong>Type:</strong></span><span class="s2"> Service Request<br /> </span><span class="s1"><strong>Category:</strong></span><span class="s2"> Account / Password Management</span></p>
<p class="p3"><strong>Scenario</strong></p>
<p class="p4">A user has forgotten their password and requests assistance restoring access to their work account.</p>
<p class="p5"><strong>Troubleshooting activities</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Verify the user’s identity.</li>
<li>Determine whether self-service password reset is available.</li>
<li>Follow the approved password-reset procedure.</li>
<li>Confirm successful authentication after the reset.</li>
<li>Avoid recording passwords or sensitive authentication information.</li>
</ul>
<p class="p3"><strong>Expected outcome</strong></p>
<p class="p4">Complete the authorized reset process and confirm that the user can access the account.</p>
<p class="p4"><strong>Skills demonstrated:</strong> Service request management, identity verification, password management, secure user support.</p>
<h3><span class="s1"><strong>Case 4 — Slow Windows 11 Computer</strong></span></h3>
<p class="p3"><strong>Ticket ID:</strong><span class="s2"> IT-004<br /> </span><span class="s1"><strong>Type:</strong></span><span class="s2"> Incident<br /> </span><span class="s1"><strong>Category:</strong></span><span class="s2"> Hardware / Performance</span></p>
<p class="p3"><strong>Scenario</strong></p>
<p class="p4">A user reports that their Windows 11 computer has become unusually slow. Applications take longer than expected to open, and normal tasks are difficult to complete.</p>
<p class="p5"><strong>Troubleshooting activities</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Review CPU, memory, and disk utilization in Task Manager.</li>
<li>Identify processes consuming unusually high resources.</li>
<li>Check available disk space.</li>
<li>Review startup applications.</li>
<li>Check for pending updates or recent software changes.</li>
<li>Record relevant findings before making changes.</li>
</ul>
<p class="p3"><strong>Expected outcome</strong></p>
<p class="p4">Identify a likely cause, perform an appropriate corrective action, and verify whether system performance improves.</p>
<p class="p4">Escalate if the evidence suggests a hardware fault or a more complex operating system issue.</p>
<p class="p4"><strong>Skills demonstrated:</strong> Windows troubleshooting, Task Manager, resource monitoring, diagnostic reasoning.</p>
<h3><span class="s1"><strong>Case 5 — Network Printer Offline</strong></span></h3>
<p class="p3"><strong>Ticket ID:</strong><span class="s2"> IT-005<br /> </span><span class="s1"><strong>Type:</strong></span><span class="s2"> Incident<br /> </span><span class="s1"><strong>Category:</strong></span><span class="s2"> Hardware / Printing</span></p>
<p class="p3"><strong>Scenario</strong></p>
<p class="p4">A user cannot print documents because a shared network printer appears offline.</p>
<p class="p5"><strong>Troubleshooting activities</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Confirm the printer’s status.</li>
<li>Check power and physical connections where applicable.</li>
<li>Verify connectivity to the printer or print server.</li>
<li>Review the print queue for stuck jobs.</li>
<li>Check the selected printer and workstation configuration.</li>
<li>Follow approved procedures before restarting services or changing settings.</li>
</ul>
<p class="p3"><strong>Expected outcome</strong></p>
<p class="p4">Restore printing if the cause is within Level 1 scope and verify successful printing with a test document.</p>
<p class="p4">Escalate if the printer, print server, or network infrastructure requires further investigation.</p>
<p class="p4"><strong>Skills demonstrated:</strong> Peripheral support, printer troubleshooting, Windows printing, user communication.</p>

<h3><span class="s1"><strong>Case 10 — Suspicious Phishing Email</strong></span></h3>
<p class="p3"><strong>Ticket ID:</strong><span class="s2"> IT-010<br /> </span><span class="s1"><strong>Type:</strong></span><span class="s2"> Incident<br /> </span><span class="s1"><strong>Category:</strong></span><span class="s2"> Security / Suspicious Email</span></p>
<p class="p3"><strong>Scenario</strong></p>
<p class="p4">A user reports receiving a suspicious email requesting urgent action through an unfamiliar link or unexpected attachment.</p>
<p class="p5"><strong>Troubleshooting activities</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Follow the organization’s security incident reporting procedure.</li>
<li>Record relevant details about the message.</li>
<li>Determine whether the user clicked a link, opened an attachment, or submitted credentials.</li>
<li>Advise the user not to interact further with the suspicious message.</li>
<li>Avoid forwarding suspicious attachments outside approved reporting channels.</li>
<li>Escalate to the security team when required.</li>
</ul>
<p class="p3"><strong>Expected outcome</strong></p>
<p class="p4">Ensure the report reaches the appropriate team and document the action taken.</p>
<p class="p4">If the user interacted with the message or submitted credentials, follow the organization’s incident escalation procedure.</p>
<p class="p6"><strong>Skills demonstrated:</strong> Security awareness, incident reporting, user communication, escalation, data protection.</p>
